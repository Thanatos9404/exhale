# Problem statement alignment

**Challenge 6, Urban Air Quality Assistant.** Build a GenAI-powered assistant that helps families and communities navigate severe winter smog and urban air pollution. It should suggest low-emission daily routines, indoor air quality hacks and sustainable alternatives tailored to location and lifestyle, while clearly explaining the direct impact of their choices.

This page traces every phrase of that statement to the feature, the code and the test that delivers it.

## One persona, served in depth

Exhale is built for one household rather than for "users in general": a three-generation family in a smog-hit Indian city.

| Who                                  | What matters for them                           | How Exhale uses it                                                            |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A working parent                     | Commutes 14 km by car, wants to keep exercising | Commute mode and departure time, exercise window                              |
| A child with asthma                  | Twice as sensitive to PM2.5                     | A lower outdoor limit (27.5 µg/m³ instead of 55), play moved or taken indoors |
| A grandmother with a heart condition | Home most of the day                            | Indoor sources, airing time, purifier sizing                                  |

This household is the default profile ([src/lib/profile/schema.ts](src/lib/profile/schema.ts)), and every field can be changed in Settings. The same family then helps its neighbourhood: one tap turns today's air into a bulletin for the residents' or school group (see "Families and communities" below).

### Before and after, on a winter morning

The rows below are the engine's own output for the default household on the repository's fixed winter-morning fixture, a Delhi-like day with a deep night-time inversion ([src/test/fixtures.ts](src/test/fixtures.ts)). [tests/workedExample.test.ts](tests/workedExample.test.ts) recomputes them and fails the build if this page drifts.

The family sees: "Smog lid 00:00 to 10:00: air can only mix 30 m high. Cleanest hour is 13:00. Today's plan cuts your household's dose by 34%."

|                                | A usual day                                       | With the Exhale plan                                                      |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What the family knows          | An AQI number                                     | When the lid lifts, which hour is cleanest, and what each choice is worth |
| Airing the house               | Air out at 08:00 · 210 µg/m³ outside              | Air out 13:00 to 14:00 · 70 µg/m³ outside                                 |
| Leaving for work               | Leave at 09:00 · 210 µg/m³ on the street          | Leave at 11:00 · 100 µg/m³ on the street                                  |
| Child's cycle ride             | Kid: cycle ride outdoors at 17:00 · 100 µg/m³     | Kid: cycle ride indoors, windows shut                                     |
| Mosquito coil                  | Mosquito coil burning 22:00 to 06:00              | Bed net or plug-in repellent: 668 µg saved, the largest single free win   |
| Commute                        | Car (petrol), 14 km each way · 4.76 kg CO2e a day | Metro + N95 · 0.87 kg CO2e a day                                          |
| PM2.5 inhaled by the household | 3,545 µg                                          | 2,334 µg: 34% less, or 7.8 cigarette-equivalents avoided                  |
| Emissions                      | As usual                                          | 19.5 kg of CO2e saved per week                                            |

The planner never keeps a change that would make anyone worse off. A property test checks this over thousands of generated households and forecasts ([src/lib/planner/guard.ts](src/lib/planner/guard.ts), [src/lib/planner/engine.property.test.ts](src/lib/planner/engine.property.test.ts)).

## Why generative AI is essential here, and where it stops

Exhale separates deciding from explaining, and uses the model for the part only a model can do.

| Job                                                          | Done by                                             | Why                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Compute doses, limits, windows and savings                   | The deterministic engine                            | Health numbers must be reproducible and testable. A language model must not invent them                                                         |
| Explain each action to this particular family in plain words | Gemini, structured output                           | A rule engine can say "move exercise to 13:00". Only a model can say why that matters for a child with asthma whose grandmother is home all day |
| Suggest hacks and swaps that fit the home                    | Gemini, structured output                           | The space of fuels, rooms, budgets and habits is too large for templates                                                                        |
| Answer follow-up questions in conversation                   | Gemini, streaming, optional Google Search grounding | Open questions need language understanding and current local advisories                                                                         |
| Keep working when the model is unavailable                   | Claude Haiku, then a rules engine                   | A health tool must not fail closed on a smoggy morning                                                                                          |

The model's output is validated against a schema and merged with the engine's numbers by action id, so the family reads the model's words next to figures the model could not change ([server/planAi.ts](server/planAi.ts)).

## What is deliberately out of scope

- **Medical advice.** Exhale gives exposure guidance and tells people with symptoms to seek care. It does not diagnose.
- **Street-level sensors.** Data comes from the CAMS atmospheric model through Open-Meteo, not from a sensor on the user's street. The app names that source next to its numbers.
- **Accounts and group features.** The community bulletin is shared through the groups people already use. Exhale keeps no accounts and stores nothing about a neighbourhood.
